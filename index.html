FAQ

Quick answers.

Straight answers about assessments, firewall and network security, monitoring, Fortinet expertise, and pricing.

Do you offer penetration testing?+
No. The main offer is cybersecurity assessment, firewall and network security, monitoring, hardening, and incident-response support. The assessment focuses on practical risk discovery and remediation, not formal pentesting.
Are you a Fortinet partner?+
No. Obserion does not claim official Fortinet partner status. The offer is based on hands-on Fortinet and FortiGate expertise, including configuration, troubleshooting, policies, VPN, IPS profiles, and reporting.
What is included in the cybersecurity assessment?+
The assessment reviews firewall rules, network exposure, user access, endpoint security, backups, remote access, logging visibility, and the biggest practical risks. You receive a clear prioritized report.
Do you provide cloud services?+
Cloud services are not a core standalone offer. If cloud accounts or services are part of the environment, they can be reviewed from a security perspective during assessment or monitoring.
How are prices calculated?+
Pricing depends on the number of devices, firewall complexity, number of locations, monitoring scope, and how much remediation is needed. The listed prices are realistic starting points for Serbian SMBs.
How can I get started?+
Start with a cybersecurity assessment. It gives a clear view of your current risk and helps decide whether you need monthly monitoring, firewall hardening, or advanced support.
