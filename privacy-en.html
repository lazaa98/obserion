Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Obserion, Pirot, Serbia. For any question about your data, write to info@obserion.com.

What we collect

The only data we collect through this website is what you send us yourself via the contact form:

  • first and last name,
  • business email address,
  • company name (optional),
  • the service you are interested in and the text of your message.

The contact form does not send data to our server — it opens your email client with a pre-filled message, and you decide whether to send it. Your message reaches us as ordinary email correspondence. This site uses no tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising tools.

Why we use it

Solely to reply to your inquiry, prepare a quote and, if you wish, schedule a scoping call. We do not add your details to marketing lists and we do not send messages you did not ask for.

Legal basis

Processing is based on your request that we contact you, i.e. on taking steps prior to a possible contract, in line with the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection and, where applicable, the GDPR.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry correspondence for as long as the conversation lasts, and at most 12 months after the last exchange if no engagement follows — then we delete it. If you become a customer, data is kept in line with the contract and legal obligations (for example accounting rules).

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The only processor is the email provider through which we receive and send mail.

Your rights

At any time you may ask to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its processing. An email to info@obserion.com is enough. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date above.
